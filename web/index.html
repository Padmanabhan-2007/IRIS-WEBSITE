    (III)

    The machinery, five moves

    No black box: every number was computed by GaussianGenerativeClassifier in plain NumPy and read back through the API.

    01

    Count

    How common is each species in the training set? That is the prior.

    02

    Centre

    The average flower of each species — a point in four dimensions.

    03

    Spread

    How the four measurements wander together. Maximum-likelihood, so divide by Nk, not Nk−1. Tint is correlation; the number is covariance.

    04

    Compare

    How surprised would species k be to meet this flower? A multivariate bell curve answers, in log-space with slogdet and a linear solve — never an explicit inverse.

    05

    Decide

    Weigh each likelihood by its prior, normalise with log-sum-exp, take the largest.
